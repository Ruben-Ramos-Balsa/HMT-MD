This collection brings together the knowledge developed in Triadic Modular Holography and Dimensional Mechanics for study and academic transfer. Its texts, programs, data, tables and digital certificates make it possible to examine the operations, reproduce the calculations and follow dependencies from the foundations to their mathematical and physical consequences.

The integral corpus and the systematic treatise are the principal references. The former assembles foundations and developments produced with the assistance of artificial intelligence systems under the authors’ direction; the latter sets out the operations and proof steps in greater detail. Two shorter, complementary routes facilitate access to this foundational material. The time-crystal monograph studies the double projection of the continuum and the relationship between periodicity and memory. The narrative exposition places the historical and conceptual problem in context and explains the significance of its mathematical and physical consequences.

The twelve thematic monographs extract constructions, proofs and applications from these references in dependency order. I generates π, φ and e, derives the fine-structure constant through two internal routes and constructs the algebraic structure of the electron. II connects action, area and information: it obtains the Planck sections, length and its gravitational coupling, Boltzmann, Barbero–Immirzi, entanglement entropy and CKM angles. III derives Catalan’s constant, permittivity and permeability; it reconstructs their constitutive function and obtains impedance, propagation speed and quantum-electrical relations.

IV develops the arithmetic origin of exceptional symmetries from the difference between sum and product. It follows the incidences of the formal kernel through Witt, Golay, Leech, Moonshine and the Monster, and articulates their extensions towards T-duality and M-theory. V obtains the Bose–Einstein and Fermi–Dirac distributions and radiation laws. VI defines a particle through persistence, classifies states and paths, and constructs the general mass law and its operator; the annex brings together states, paths, masses and widths.

VII proves compatibility and restitution between action, length, gravitation, propagation, mass and information while preserving the order of transformations. VIII develops the gravitational and torsional response, field equations and the transition from contraction to expansion. IX connects primes, the zeta function and Weil positivity. X constructs positive Grassmannian and amplituhedral representations, lattice duality and the Yang–Mills mass gap, with their length and area readings. XI constructs the real line discretely and classifies its internal subsets within holographic closure. XII determines the holographic constant and the laws of information conservation, recovery and reversibility.

The collection makes an extensive investigation accessible in the service of mathematical and physical knowledge. It is offered for study, reproduction, assessment and academic use, and as a basis for possible scientific publications. The exposition connects operations, objects and relations with their physical realisations and metrological comparison; the proofs and executable materials allow the content, its provenance and its scope to be studied together.
