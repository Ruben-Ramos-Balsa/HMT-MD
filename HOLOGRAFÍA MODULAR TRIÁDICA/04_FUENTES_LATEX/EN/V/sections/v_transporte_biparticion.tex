\subsection{Spectral entropy and preservation under transport}
\label{v:ant:sec:biparticion-transporte}

The entropy of a reduced state depends on its spectrum and on the
bipartition defining the reduction. To make the continuation
toward duality precise, let \(\mathcal H_A,\mathcal H_B\) be the two factors
and let \(\rho_{AB}\) be a state with finite entropies. A factorized
transport \(U_A\otimes U_B\) produces
\[
 \rho'_A=U_A\rho_AU_A^*,\qquad S(\rho'_A)=S(\rho_A).
\]
The first identity follows from the definition of the partial trace, and the
second from spectral invariance. For a general unitary transport,
the equivalent formulation also transports
the inclusion of the subsystem's algebra of observables. This specification
identifies the geometric datum that must accompany a claim
of entanglement preservation.
