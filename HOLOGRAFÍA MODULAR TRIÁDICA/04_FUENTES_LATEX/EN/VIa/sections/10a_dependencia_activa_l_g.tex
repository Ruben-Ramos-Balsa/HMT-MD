\section{Structural length, action, and gravitational response}
\label{sec:vi-catalogo-dependencia-l-g}

The catalogue brings together \(324\) routes and \(855\) metrological
records. Their interpretation follows the construction of the same HMT--MD
state, in the causal order
\[
 \mathrm{APP}\longrightarrow\mathrm{TRIT}\longrightarrow\mathrm{TPK}
 \longrightarrow X_{\mathrm{enr}}
 \longrightarrow\mathcal C^{\mathrm{disc}}_{\mathrm{joint}}
 \longrightarrow L_{\alpha}
 \longrightarrow(H\Lambda,R\Lambda)
 \longrightarrow G .
 \tag{A.1}\label{eq:vi-cat-cadena-lg}
\]
The coordinates \(\pi\), \(\varphi\), \(e\), and
\(\alpha_{\mathrm{HMT}}\) are correlated outputs of the preceding typed
nonadic image; they are not entered as conventional values to select this
realization. For \(N=12\cdot4\cdot120=5760\), the marked length is
\[
 L_{\alpha}=\alpha_{\mathrm{HMT}}^{16}
 \frac{N-1}{4N}L_*
 =\frac{5759}{23040}\alpha_{\mathrm{HMT}}^{16}L_* .
 \tag{A.2}\label{eq:vi-cat-longitud-alpha}
\]

In this section we abbreviate \(\hbar=\hbar_{\mathrm{ret}}\) and
\(c=c_{\mathrm{int}}\). On the nonzero positive fibre, the energy \(H\),
reduced action length \(\Lambda\), and conjugate radial readout \(R\) satisfy
the following two operator relations. We also define \(M:=H/c^2\) and declare
the physical radial identification \(R:=GM/c^2\). Comparing it, on the
invertible fibre, with the operator relation
\(R=L_{\alpha}^{2}H/(\hbar c)\) gives
\[
 H\Lambda=\hbar c\,I,\qquad
 R\Lambda=L_{\alpha}^{2}I,\qquad
 R=\frac{L_{\alpha}^{2}}{\hbar c}H,\qquad
 \boxed{G=\frac{c^{3}L_{\alpha}^{2}}{\hbar}} .
 \tag{A.3}\label{eq:vi-cat-realizacion-lg}
\]
Metrological recognition of \(G\) is posterior and takes no part in selecting
the state, the readout operator, or its coefficients.

\subsection{Record M10 and preservation of the mass law}
\label{sec:vi-catalogo-m10-activo}

Code M10 in the documentary repertoire refers to the typed
realization in \eqref{eq:vi-cat-realizacion-lg}. M10 neither postulates a
gravitational particle nor turns the external record retained by the inventory
under \texttt{GRAVITATIONAL\_OR\_HYPOTHETICAL} into an HMT output: that record
remains external metrological data, separate from the
\(L_{\alpha}\mapsto G\) construction.

The energy scale induced by the same length is
\[
 Q_L:=\frac{\hbar c}{L_{\alpha}}.
 \tag{A.4}\label{eq:vi-cat-escala-q}
\]
If an equivalent chart replaces \(Q_L\) by \(Q_L'\), the electronic
coefficient co-transforms according to
\[
 b_e'=b_e\frac{Q_L}{Q_L'},\qquad Q_L'b_e'=Q_Lb_e .
 \tag{A.5}\label{eq:vi-cat-cotransformacion-be}
\]
The definitions of the clock energy and electronic factor give
\[
 E_{\mathrm{clk}}=Q_L,\qquad
 m_{\mathrm{clk}}=\frac{E_{\mathrm{clk}}}{c^2},\qquad
 b_e=\frac{E_e^{(0)}}{E_{\mathrm{clk}}},\qquad
 m_{\mathrm{clk}}b_e=\frac{E_e^{(0)}}{c^2}.
\]
The exact cancellation of \(Q_L\) proves that the realization of \(G\) does
not derive the masses anew, select routes, or modify the tables already
constructed. The specialization \(\Xi_\Gamma=1\) in the compatibility theorem
is equivalent to \(\bar\lambda_\Gamma=R_{108}=L_{\alpha}\): it identifies a
coincidence of lengths and neither asserts nor creates the existence of a
Planck-mass species in the catalogue.

The \(10^{-34}\) decade belongs exclusively to the absolute
action--clock--mass branch,
\(\hbar=\eta_{\mathrm{ret}}10^{-34}\mathcal U_S\); it is not reinserted into
the relative exponent, the route character, or a species correction.

\subsection{Constitutive channels and the oriented Catalan moment}
\label{sec:vi-catalogo-propietario-constitutivo}

Let \(0<q_+,q_-<1\) be the labelled transport channels and \(P_+,P_-\)
their orthogonal sheet projectors. For \(s\in(0,1)\), define
\[
 \mathcal C_2(s)=\sum_{k\geq0}
 \frac{(-1)^k s^{2k+1}}{(2k+1)^2},\qquad
 \mathcal D=s\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm ds},\qquad
 f(s)=\frac{1-s^3}{1-s^4}.
 \tag{A.6}\label{eq:vi-cat-constitutivas}
\]
Local uniform convergence of the series and its derivatives permits two
applications of \(\mathcal D\). The resulting geometric series gives
\[
 \mathcal D^2\mathcal C_2(s)=\frac{s}{1+s^2},\qquad
 f(s)=\frac{1+s+s^2}{s(1+s)}\mathcal D^2\mathcal C_2(s).
\]
The identities \(90=3\cdot30\) and \(120=4\cdot30\) determine
\[
 s_{\pm}=q_{\pm}^{30},\qquad
 r_{\pm}=f(s_{\pm})
 =\frac{1-q_{\pm}^{90}}{1-q_{\pm}^{120}}.
\]
Thus \(V=r_+P_++r_-P_-\) combines both responses while retaining their
labels. The \(\mathcal C_2\) series converges absolutely at \(s=1\); its
value is \(G_{\mathrm{Cat}}\), distinguished from the gravitational
coefficient \(G\).

The full function is recovered from \(f\) by
\[
 \mathcal C_2(s)=\int_0^s\log\!\left(\frac{s}{t}\right)
 \frac{1+t}{1+t+t^2}f(t)\,\mathrm dt.
\]
Indeed, \((1+t)f(t)/(1+t+t^2)=1/(1+t^2)\); applying \(\mathcal D\) twice
gives \(s/(1+s^2)\). Homogeneous solutions have the form \(a\log s+b\), and
\(\mathcal C_2(s)\to0\) as \(s\downarrow0\) forces both coefficients to
vanish. The response, its regular recovery, and the meaning of the Catalan
coordinate used in the catalogue entries are thereby explicit.
