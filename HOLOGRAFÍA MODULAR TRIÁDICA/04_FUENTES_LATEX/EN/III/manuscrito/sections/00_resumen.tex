\noindent
A positive, invertible constitutive response of the electromagnetic
vacuum is constructed: two oriented channels jointly determine the
dimensionless coordinates of permittivity, permeability, impedance and
propagation speed. Their exact relations and the unique recovery of the
angular pair producing them are proved. The same response reconstructs
the shape of the action ellipse and expresses the Barbero--Immirzi
functional through its two labelled eigenvalues. The result thus relates
the determination of values to the structural information preserved by
their evaluation.

The construction starts from a formal mathematical core called Triadic
Modular Holography, henceforth HMT. \emph{All Possible Paths} (APP) is
the arithmetic path structure on the toroidal support
$(\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z)^2$; its cardinal displacements form the
additive Cayley graph, and its sheets preserve the sum and product
evaluations with residue and quotient. TRIT is the oriented signature
$\{-1,0,+1\}$ distinguishing local regimes. The
\emph{Topological Phase Kernel} (TPK) composes selection, transport,
memory update and return on this enriched state. Nonadic prolongation
and dodecaphasic closure generate the correlated coordinates and
records from which the action, angular pair and incidences used by the
response arise.

The sectors $90$ and $120$ of the transport $T$ determine
$\mathcal V=(I-T^{90})(I-T^{120})^{-1}$. Its scalar function
$f(s)=(1+s+s^2)/(1+s+s^2+s^3)$ is a decreasing bijection from $(0,1)$
onto $(3/4,1)$: this property establishes cubic inversion and its
domain. Sheet interchange permutes permittivity and permeability,
inverts impedance and preserves speed. The identity between
constitutive speed and the clock's kinematic speed is also proved
in the same dimensional chart.

Realization on the twelve-phase cycle supplies the trace defect
$-1/12$, a representation of $f$ as a quotient of determinants and a quarter-turn
whose square is $-I$. Double logarithmic integration of its resolvent
produces the Catalan moment. At the Pell section $2-\sqrt3$, an exact
identity with Catalan's constant and $\log(2+\sqrt3)$ is proved;
the two constitutive arguments retain their own angular determination.
Inversion of $f$ subsequently composes the vacuum response with the
oriented Barbero--Immirzi functional.
Integral reconstruction also proves that the constitutive function
determines the complete oriented moment under the condition
$\mathcal C_2(s)\to0$ at the origin; its limit at $s=1$ recovers
Catalan's constant. A functional correspondence, with its orientation
and arguments, is thereby preserved alongside its evaluations.

Discrete polarization is obtained from the bounded vacancy balance,
and the electronic prefactor $\sqrt3/4$ from the commutator norm of
projections associated with the sum and product fibres of the
three-dimensional arithmetic support. The positive
charge section, constructed from $\alpha$, action and impedance,
determines the von Klitzing, conductance, magnetic flux and Josephson
relations, including their cross-identities and covariance under action
return. Dimensional realizations preserve the action, charge and speed
sections and their changes of units. The numerical evaluation
concerns the internal coordinates; physical comparison explicitly
retains the representation and dimensional chart used.

Positive separation of the common and oriented modes specifies the
information preserved by unimodular normalization. Fourier resolution
of the dodecaphasic incidence identifies four modes and their exact
weights, distinguishing the rank defect, signed multiplicities and
singular coefficient of return.

The inversion also induces an associative composition law for responses,
with an additive angular coordinate. In the tree-level electroweak
realisation, the gauge and Higgs readers jointly recover the channels
and impedance within an explicit domain.
