\subsection{Persistent identity, atlas, and conjugation}

Persistence is formulated over compatible extensions; the route
preserves its observable history; the representation determines which
sheet, orientation, spin, flavour, and colour data distinguish states.
This identity precedes the mass operator and relates its readings without
reducing the particle to a numerical coordinate.

The atlas provides a finite classification that can be traversed
in full. Its \(81\) cells, \(56\) collections, \(13\) multisections and
\(324\) oriented conditions are four censuses of different objects.
Their organisation identifies the central exception of the electron and the
non-central fibres. In the latter, preserving a multisection or an
operator is a positive description of the result: it specifies what remains
equivariant and which additional operation produces a spectral reading.
Physical individualisation takes place through its representation and
channel, preserving this distinction.

Conjugation likewise does not reduce to a single sign inversion.
Cellular inversion, reversal of a route, the involution on
words, charge conjugation and spinorial holonomy preserve
their own domains and actions. The Möbius construction specifies the
fibre and loop that generate orientation inversion. Return to the
same phase may leave different memory, while restoration of a
representation may require an additional circuit. These results
describe how structure persists when its partial observations
coincide.
Antiunitary conjugation additionally requires a dynamical distinction:
commutation with the Hamiltonian reverses the direction of the evolution
group, whereas preservation of its real subspace at all times
requires the corresponding anticommutation relation. Compatibility
with the mass operator retains its own formulation.

\subsection{Mass evaluation, composition, and occupation}

Mass evaluation combines two levels. The refined character uses
the integer signature and channel moments; the dimensional section
composes action, clock and unit chart. The electronic prefactor
remains present when expressing the electron relative to the chronological
scale. The ratio of action sections acts in the return
readers and allows fibres to be compared through exponents derived from
their records. Equality of the two central readers has received
a concrete proof on the electronic trajectory, with its scope
distinguished from that of the other atlas profiles.
Clock velocity and constitutive velocity coincide through
the identity between the product of the two channels and the determinant of the
response operator. Changing the unit chart transforms their
coordinates while leaving the dimensional quantity invariant.

Particle composition has been placed before signature
extraction. Binding records preserve boundary conditions,
sheets and predecessors on which the functionals act. The binding
term therefore has a defined place in the composite
operator. Mesons and baryons are organised through their constituent
spaces and chromatic representations; resonances
additionally require open-channel dynamics and the definition of the
pole. Their width and survival time are coordinated readings
of that dynamics.

In the neutral antipodal sector, the extractor's two scalar contractions
vanish exactly: \(b_{120}^{\nu}=b_{\zeta}^{\nu}=0\). This cancellation
does not remove the oriented register, because the anti-invariant subspace
retains the complex operator \(J^2=-I\); nor does it extend to general
routes or modify masses or PMNS transport. In the longitudinal
prolongation, comparison of ten \(108\)-step words with their
\(1080\)-step concatenation includes the explicit seam term. The new
boundaries are not crowns, so that
\(\Delta C_z=0\) and \(\Delta k_{\Delta_4}=\Delta T_z\), with \(-2\) an
attainable local-control value. Representation periodicity and the memory
of the complete TPK state therefore remain distinct.

The graded completion develops occupation over the complete
modes already declared. Antisymmetrisation constructs the
CAR, from which Pauli exclusion follows. Symmetrisation
constructs the CCR on the finite-particle domain and makes visible
the boundary term introduced by a bosonic truncation.
The distinction between equality of a mass and coincidence of a mode
is expressed by the Gram determinant of double occupation.
Spin, sheet or colour may preserve independent modes even when
a partial observable has the same value.

Orbital realisations connect action to closure conditions
and Kepler--Sommerfeld geometry. Their metric,
central-dynamics and representation assumptions have been retained alongside the
equations that use them. In the fusion and braiding sectors,
the incidence ring, its positive dimensions,
association-change matrices and exchange characters are made explicit,
together with the local identities and realisation conditions
used by each. This organisation makes it possible to recognise what belongs to
an algebraic construction, a dynamical realisation and a
physical identification.

\subsection{From the spectrum to metrological comparison}

Metrological comparison completes the progression through typed
observables. The sector-by-sector comparisons are accompanied by
the complete inventories and their sources. A running mass, a pole
mass, a limit and a width retain their definitions, units
and uncertainties. Each model evaluation is linked to its record,
reader and chart; each experimental observation preserves its edition
and provenance. The \(855\) documentary mass and width rows
constitute the domain of the comparison catalogue, distinct from the number
of individualised predictions.

The contribution brings together three determinations: the object that
persists, the operation producing its spectrum, and the chart in which
that spectrum is compared. Mass results from their composition;
orientation, memory, and fibre structure remain available to distinguish
states sharing a partial reading. The electronic case realises that
composition in an explicit scalar section, while the atlas preserves the
diversity of operator-valued sectors and their physical realisations.
This relationship between structure and magnitude unifies the result.
