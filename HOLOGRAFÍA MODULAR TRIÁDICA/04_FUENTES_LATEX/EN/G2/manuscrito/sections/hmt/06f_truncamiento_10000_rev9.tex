\section{Certification at depth \texorpdfstring{\(10^4\)}{10^4} of the coinductive expansions of \texorpdfstring{\(\pi\), \(e\) and \(\varphi\)}{pi, e, and phi}}
\label{sec:truncamiento-10000-pi-e-phi-rev9}

\noindent\textbf{Mathematical scope.}
The preceding uniform theorem quantifies over every positive integer \(N\) and
constructs the infinite object as the inverse limit of its compatible cylinders.
This chapter runs the case \(N=10^4\) as a particularly demanding finite
certificate; \(10^4\) is not a bound on the generator, just as a
million-digit run would be another instance of the same algorithm, not a
modification of its law.

The present execution fixes a computational depth and checks that the
resulting cylinder is contained in a single decimal cell of ten thousand
positions. The smallest number of blocks compatible with the nonadic monodromy
and with
\[
  3^{6B}>10^{10000}
\]
is
\[
  B=3501=389\cdot9,
  \qquad 6B=21006\ \text{trits}.
\]

For each of the three channels, the \(729\) subcylinders are exhaustively
traversed at every stage, and the unique subcylinder compatible with the reader's
rational bracket is retained. The calculation yields
\[
 \begin{array}{c|r}
 \text{quantity calculated per channel}&\text{value}\\
 \hline
 \text{complete partitions }729\to1&3501\\
 \text{transported trits}&21006\\
 \text{pairs of equal phase }(K,K+9)&3492\\
 \text{complete nonadic turns}&388
 \end{array}
\]
and does not require an additional nonad. The first \(396\) blocks
coincide exactly with the preceding thousand-digit witness.

\Needspace{28\baselineskip}
The closure, propagation, and self-scaling readers are evaluated by means of
exact rational inequalities. Computational acceleration reorganizes the sums
through integer arithmetic and does not open libraries of constants or target
decimal prefixes. Only after the outputs have been written is a second,
independent computation performed. The following are verified, in this order:
the rational generation of the three channels, the G9--T1 composition of
\(\alpha_{\rm HMT}\), and the agreement of all ten thousand digits with the
reference expansions consulted only at the end. The three comparisons are
exact at all ten thousand computed positions. This finite verification does
not replace the uniform argument for prolongation to arbitrary depth.

The expanded proof also makes the meaning of return precise. Across the \(3492\) pairs per channel, the phase is preserved and the complete state always changes, since depth, prefix, and cylinder advance. A reduced local projection may recur in some pairs without the TPK state returning. This distinction replaces the stronger claim of local aperiodicity with the property actually proved: phase monodromy with memory of the enriched state.

The four complete expansions are printed in
\cref{app:desarrollos-10000-rev9}. The recurrences, rational
intervals, and preceding counts determine the calculation; the printed expansions
are its finite truncations and make it possible to compare each digit without
becoming premises of the generator.
