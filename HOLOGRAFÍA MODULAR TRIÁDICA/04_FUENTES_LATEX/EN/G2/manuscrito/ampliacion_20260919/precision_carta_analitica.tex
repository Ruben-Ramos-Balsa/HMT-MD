\subsection*{Determination of the correlated analytic extension}

The complete construction of the analytic chart fixes the following dependency order. The enriched state $\mathsf s$ first expresses the pre-coordinate
\[
 a(\mathsf s)=p(\mathsf s)+e_0(\mathsf s)-f(\mathsf s)
              -\kappa_{\rm per}(\mathsf s),
\]
where $p=\pi_{\rm HMT}-3$, $e_0=e_{\rm HMT}-2$ and $f=\varphi_{\rm HMT}-1$. The three regional coordinates and the periodic reading of the register belong to the same generative development. Integer normalization and the analytic chart realize two correlated expressions of this pre-coordinate.

With $q=1/729$ and $E_{9,\mathsf s}$ the ninth-order jet, the linking coefficient is
\[
 \lambda(\mathsf s)=
 -\frac{E_{9,\mathsf s}(a(\mathsf s))
         \bigl(1-q\,a(\mathsf s)^3\bigr)}{a(\mathsf s)^{10}}.
\]
The complete extension is then determined by
\[
 E_{\mathsf s,\infty}(X)=E_{9,\mathsf s}(X)
      +\lambda(\mathsf s)\frac{X^{10}}{1-X^3/729},
\]
or, equivalently, by the recurrence of all its coefficients:
\[
 b_{10+3n}=729^{-n}\lambda(\mathsf s),\qquad
 b_{11+3n}=b_{12+3n}=0\qquad(n\geq0).
\]

Uniqueness of $\lambda$ has as its domain the class of extensions $E_{9,\mathsf s}(X)+\mu X^{10}/(1-qX^3)$: the condition of vanishing at $a(\mathsf s)$ determines $\mu=\lambda(\mathsf s)$ exactly. Naturality of truncations, the geometric remainder and derivative bounds then establish convergence and the unique simple root. This root coincides with the integer expression at every depth.

This is the precise status of the double realization: identity of the arithmetic and analytic expressions of the same state, with explicit dependence on the generated pre-coordinate and the geometric extension class. The construction is developed in full in Section~\ref{x_reciprocidad:subsec:alpha-lector-completo-rev08}, with its proofs of naturality, convergence and compatibility.
