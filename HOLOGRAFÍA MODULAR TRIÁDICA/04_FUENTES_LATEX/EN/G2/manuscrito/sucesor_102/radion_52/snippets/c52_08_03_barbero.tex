\subsubsection{Barbero--Immirzi hexad--octad functional}

\paragraph{Oriented series and incidence jump}

We define
\begin{equation}
S_{90}(q)=\frac{q^{90}}{1-q^{270}},
\qquad
S_{120}(q)=\frac{q^{120}}{1-q^{360}},
\label{eq:barbero-series}
\end{equation}
and
\[
\Delta S_m=S_m(q_-)-S_m(q_+).
\]
The marked inclusion functional is
\begin{equation}
K_{6\to8}=12\Delta S_{90}-\Delta S_{120}.
\label{eq:barbero-núcleo}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Diophantine verification of the dodecaphasic weight \(12:-1\)}

The dodecaphasic fundamental chain and its return reader, constructed in
\eqref{eq:l9s102:barbero-cadena-dodecafasica}--%
\eqref{eq:l9s102:barbero-peso-generado}, produce twelve terms of type
\(S_{90}\) and one boundary term of type \(-S_{120}\). The oriented pair
is determined before calculating the pole coefficient.

Consider \(aS_{90}-bS_{120}\). Verification of the coefficient of
\((1-q)^{-1}\) takes the form
\begin{equation}
\frac{a}{270}-\frac{b}{360}=\frac1{24}.
\label{eq:pole-condition}
\end{equation}
Multiplying by \(1080\) gives
\[
4a-3b=45.
\]
The unique seam of the fundamental turn has absolute multiplicity one.
Its Diophantine verification corresponds to the minimal integer counterterm
\(|b|=1\). For \(b=1\), \(a=12\); for \(b=-1\), \(a=21/2\) is not an integer.
Therefore,
\begin{equation}
\boxed{a:b=12:1,\qquad aS_{90}-bS_{120}=12S_{90}-S_{120}.}
\label{eq:12minus1}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Uniqueness of the Diophantine verification]
The generated pair \((12,1)\) is the unique integer solution of
\eqref{eq:pole-condition} with a positive principal channel and minimal
counterterm \(|b|=1\).
\end{proposition}
