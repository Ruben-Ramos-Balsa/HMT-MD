\section{Conclusion}

The theory obtains a unified response to the initial intuition. An
orbit is a helical sheet when depth is preserved; the
bidirectional involution produces conjugate sheets of ascent and descent; the
nested circles are level sections; the cylinders are families of those
sections. The classical spirals are not selected as templates. They appear upon
publishing in \(\R^2\) distinct radial characters of an
APP--TRIT--TPK history.

The principal result is not a new list of curves. It is a structural
separation between genealogy and appearance, accompanied by a reader that
preserves exactly the information that appearance can forget. The curve is
placed at the end. The real line, the real plane, and Frenet geometries
are publications. The phase may return while the memory advances;
the circle may be intrinsic or projected; the logarithmic spiral
may be the exact shadow of a spectral screw; and the same mechanism
may admit Archimedean, electromagnetic, and material realizations without
conflating their codomains.

\begin{resultbox}[title={Functorial classification of the radial reader and stratification of its realizations}]
The APP--TRIT--TPK composition generates a class of radial histories with
holonomy, orientation, carry, boundary, and memory. The enriched reader is
natural under truncation and equivariant under nonadic holonomy. Its
Archimedean publication produces power--logarithmic curves and helical
suspensions, but it is not faithful: the same figure may preserve distinct
genealogies. This is the intimate dynamical structure of space that the
conventional classification of curves observes only after projection.
\end{resultbox}
