\subsection{Universal embedding of an APP signal into a quantum Markov semigroup}

The cyclic constructions use only the Latin symbol. A general framework
exists for introducing any Hermitian signal of the two sheets without
disrupting the magic sums.

\begin{proposition}[Double centering of an operatorial signal]
\label{gmc:prop:doble-centrado}
Let $G=(G_{ij})_{i,j=0}^{8}$ be a grid of self-adjoint matrices in
$M_3$. Define
\[
 H_{ij}=G_{ij}
 -\frac19\sum_{j'}G_{ij'}
 -\frac19\sum_{i'}G_{i'j}
 +\frac1{81}\sum_{i',j'}G_{i'j'}.
 \tag{GMC-9.5}
\]
Then all row and column sums of $H$ vanish. If
$M=\max_{i,j}\|H_{ij}\|$ and $\varepsilon\in\mathbb R$ satisfies
\[
 |\varepsilon|\le\frac1{9M}
 \tag{GMC-9.6}
\]
(with the convention that there is no restriction if $M=0$), the matrix
\[
 A_{ij}=\frac19I_3+\varepsilon H_{ij}
 \tag{GMC-9.7}
\]
is a \QMS of sizes $(9,3)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Upon summing \textup{(GMC-9.5)} along a row or column, the four terms cancel.
Therefore, \textup{(GMC-9.7)} sums to $I_3$ on each line. Since
$-MI_3\preceq H_{ij}\preceq MI_3$, the bound \textup{(GMC-9.6)} implies
$A_{ij}\succeq0$.
\end{proof}

To use sources of different dimensions, a UCP reader into $M_3$ must first be declared: if $T_{ij}=T_{ij}^*$ belongs to the source operator system and $\Theta$ is that reader, one sets $G_{ij}=\Theta(T_{ij})$. The qutrit images of signals constructed with $P_\Sigma$, $P_\Pi$, the block $B_c$, or route readers can thereby be inserted; operators of sizes $81\times81$ or $2\times2$ are not inserted directly as cells of $M_3$. Double centering removes every purely ``row only + column only'' component; this provides an immediate falsifier for encodings that appear coupled without possessing a two-dimensional interaction.

\begin{controlbox}
The \Cref{gmc:prop:doble-centrado} proves the existence of an APP-marked \QMS,
not its fidelity or nonsemiclassicality. The small amplitude may keep it
within a semiclassical neighborhood. The next stage must solve the SDP
\textup{(GMC-8.6)} or produce a dual witness.
\end{controlbox}

\subsection{Naturality, cofinality, and separation hypotheses required by the reconstruction theorem}

\begin{itemize}
