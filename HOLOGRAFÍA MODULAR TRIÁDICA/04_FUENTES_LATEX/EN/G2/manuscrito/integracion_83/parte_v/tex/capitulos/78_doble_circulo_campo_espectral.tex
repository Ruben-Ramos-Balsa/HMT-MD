\chapter{Cayley–Pontryagin characterization of critical zeros and effective reconstruction of the Weil spectral field}
\label{chap:hmt83-doble-circulo-campo-espectral}

Once the critical line has been located by positive factorization, the Cayley transform expresses the heights as phases on a first circle. The Pontryagin intertwiner then transports them to the nonadic circle without identifying phase return with state return. The double-circle theorem and orbit quantization are articulated with the effective reconstruction of the spectral field. The exposition explicitly preserves the hypotheses, Galerkin residuals, memory cocycle, twisted transport and the intertwiner that identifies both realizations without confusing the turn index with the spectral height.
