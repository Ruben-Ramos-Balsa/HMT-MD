\section{Reduction of Feigenbaum universality to the hyperbolic certificate of basin membership and transversality}
\label{sec:p3-final:cierre-feigenbaum}

The HMT Feigenbaum family has analytic profile, quadratic maximum,
negative Schwarzian, and certified superstable approximants up to level
\(8\). Its promotion to universality requires the certificate
\[
 \ell_u(\partial_t f_t)\ne0
\]
together with membership in the hyperbolic basin of the fixed point. Tangency,
departure from the basin, or the presence of a second unstable eigenvalue are
complete falsifiers.
