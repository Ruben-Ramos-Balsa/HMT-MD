\section{Composition of the holonomy \(G_9\) with the chart \(T_1\) and arbitrary-precision certificate for \(\alpha_{\rm HMT}\)}
\label{sec:alpha-t1-10000-rev9}

The T1 prolongation applies the carry recurrence to the triads
\(P_k,E_k,\Phi_k\) previously produced by G9 and to the declared
dodecaphasic coordinate \(K\):
\begin{align}
 s_k&=P_k+E_k-\Phi_k-K_{k\bmod12}+c_{k+1},\\
 A_k&=s_k\bmod1000,\\
 c_k&=\left\lfloor\frac{s_k}{1000}\right\rfloor.
 \label{eq:composicion-g9-t1-alpha-10000-rev9}
\end{align}
The input domain contains the ternary outputs generated by G9; it does not
contain a decimal expansion of \(\alpha\), a value of
\(\alpha^{-1}\), CODATA, or a target metrological condition.

The cylinders of \(3501\) blocks fix \(10020\) guard digits in each
channel, equivalent to \(3340\) triads. The calculation propagates, from right to
left, all possible terminal conditions in the stable set
\[
 \mathcal C=\{-2,-1,0,1,2\}.
\]
The five branches coalesce in a common prefix of \(3339\) triads, that is,
\(10017\) digits. Consequently, the first ten thousand digits of
\(\alpha_{\rm HMT}\) are independent of the carry situated beyond the
guard zone. The published output does not impose \(c_N=0\).

Causal independence can be checked after the construction: the first three thousand digits agree with the decimal expansion obtained by the external reader, but none of them enters \eqref{eq:composicion-g9-t1-alpha-10000-rev9}. The ten-thousand-digit expansion and the finite data that make it possible to repeat the calculation are presented in the appendix~\ref{app:desarrollos-10000-rev9}.
