The argument begins with compatibility of prefixes and frames,
constructs the ternary code, and determines the flag through \(K\) and the
signed record of \(\alpha\). The marked incidence then selects
the lattice origin and the Leech neighbour. The final step realizes the vertex
algebra and its automorphisms. At each stage, the structure preserved will be specified:
linear combinations, supports, discriminant classes, bilinear
form, or graded product. Explanations of the role of \(K\),
the normalization, and the origin of norm \(54\) accompany their respective
constructions in Sections \ref{exc:bandera} and \ref{exc:leech}.
