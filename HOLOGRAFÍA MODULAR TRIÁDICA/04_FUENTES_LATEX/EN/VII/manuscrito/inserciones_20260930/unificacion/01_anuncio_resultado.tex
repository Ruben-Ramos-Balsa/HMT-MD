The same composition extends to a total action of geometry, gauge and matter: it jointly incorporates gravitation and the strong, weak and electromagnetic interactions, preserving the chiral representations and the previously generated couplings. Its spin current, total metric source and torsional cross-terms between species are obtained. The Legendre transformation and symmetry identities produce joint first-class constraints and their propagation. On their regular surface, the curvature of the total canonical connection is calculated and shown to vanish in the characteristic deformation directions. The result preserves the boundary conditions and the possible global holonomy of the enriched state.
