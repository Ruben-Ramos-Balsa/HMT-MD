\section{Primitive returns and lifted frequency}
\subsection{Local extension automaton}
Let \(\mathscr X_\Gamma\) be the finite set of local states compatible with the route. A state retains

\[
 x=(t,g,B,p,\varepsilon,c,w),
\]
where \(t\) is discrete time, \(g\) the nonadic phase, \(B\) the block, \(p\) the prefix, \(\varepsilon\) the sheet, \(c\) the carry, and \(w\) the winding. An edge \(x\to y\) exists when \(y\) is an admissible extension of \(x\), respects the genealogical record, and survives \(G_9\) pruning. The table of 36,864 carry compositions supplies the local addition law; the return tables determine the memory.

Let \(U_\Gamma\) be the normalized transfer operator of this graph and \(R_\Gamma\) the orientation involution. The required identities are

\[
 U_{C_M\Gamma}=J U_\Gamma J^{-1},\qquad
 R_{C_M\Gamma}=-J R_\Gamma J^{-1}.
\]
\Needspace{7\baselineskip}
\subsection{Oriented primitive counts}
For \(n\ge1\), the oriented trace

\[
 a_n(\Gamma)=\operatorname{Tr}(R_\Gamma U_\Gamma^n)
\]
counts signed closed returns. Möbius inversion separates primitive orbits:

\[
 p_n(\Gamma)
 =\frac1n\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)a_{n/d}(\Gamma).
\]
The electron is taken as the affine origin of the relative corrections, and one defines

\[
 \boxed{
 \eta_h(\Gamma)=p_h(\Gamma)-p_h(\Gamma_e),
 \qquad h\in\mathfrak S.}
\]
This gives the map

\[
 \mathcal F_{\rm tower}:
 \Gamma^{\rm enr}\longmapsto
 \eta(\Gamma)=(\eta_h(\Gamma))_{h\in\mathfrak S}.
\]
There are no species-specific parameters. The same automaton, involution, and Möbius inversion act on all 324 routes.

\Needspace{7\baselineskip}
\subsection{Convergence of primitive refinement}
Let \(N_\Gamma=\dim\mathscr X_\Gamma\). If \(U_\Gamma\) is a contraction,

\[
 |a_n(\Gamma)|\le N_\Gamma.
\]
The number of divisors of \(n\) grows sublinearly, hence

\[
 |p_n(\Gamma)|
 \le\frac{N_\Gamma\tau(n)}n.
\]
Since \(0<q_+<q_-<1\),

\[
 |s_h|\le q_-^h+q_+^h\le2q_-^h.
\]
By comparison,

\[
 \sum_{h\in\mathfrak S}|\eta_h(\Gamma)s_h|<\infty
\]
uniformly on every finite collection of fibres. The refined character is therefore well defined.

\Needspace{7\baselineskip}
\subsection{Control identities}
The construction must satisfy the following conditions simultaneously:

\begin{enumerate}
\item invariance under relabelling of states;
\item covariance under sheet conjugation;
\item compatibility with direct sums of components;
\item equality between recomputation from the genealogical record and from the certified graph;
\item preservation of the origin \(\eta(\Gamma_e)=0\);
\item complete insensitivity to permutations of the external mass columns;
\item determination of the tails by a bound established before comparison.
\end{enumerate}
Exact representation of a given residual proves completeness of the codomain, but does not replace these seven tests.

\subsection{Integer returns do not suffice to distinguish species}
In the atlas of 324 routes, the first cell return is 27, the kinematic return within CT--108 is 54, and the extended kinematic return is 216. Being common to the routes, these integers fix the global temporal architecture, but cannot by themselves account for mass differences between species.

\subsection{Temporal holonomy}
The specific information resides in the holonomy of the extension operator. Let \(\lambda_j(\Gamma)\) be a nontrivial eigenvalue of \(U_\Gamma\) on the persistent subspace. Write

\[
 \lambda_j(\Gamma)=r_j(\Gamma)e^{i\theta_j(\Gamma)}.
\]
The associated internal frequency is

\[
 \omega_j(\Gamma)
 =\frac{\theta_j(\Gamma)+2\pi w_j(\Gamma)}{108t_0},
\]
where the integer \(w_j\) is fixed by the recorded winding, not by a subsequent branch choice. For a stable mode, \(r_j=1\); for a resonance, \(r_j<1\), and its attenuation supplies a width.

The ratio

\[
 \rho_{\omega,j}(\Gamma)
 =\frac{\omega_j(\Gamma)}{\omega_0}
\]
is dimensionless and can modify mass ratios without changing the common decade \(-34\). The energy and mass of the mode are

\[
 E_j(\Gamma)=\hbar_{\rm ret}\omega_j(\Gamma)
 \chi_{\rm full}(\Gamma)R_{\rm act}^{\kappa_j(\Gamma)},
\]
\[
 m_j(\Gamma)=E_j(\Gamma)c_{\rm int}^{-2}.
\]
This equation brings together action, clock, character, and reader without identifying any of them with another. Its numerical application requires determining \(U_\Gamma\), fixing the winding, and specifying the dimensional chart.
