\noindent
An extension of the discrete structure of the continuum is constructed
that relates its numerical coordinates \(\pi,\varphi,e,\alpha\) to exceptional incidence,
vertex operator algebras and the duality of a compact sector.
Triadic Modular Holography (HMT) begins with \emph{All Possible Paths}
(APP): the support \((\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z)^2\), its additive Cayley graph
with cardinal-direction generators \(\pm(1,0),\pm(0,1)\), and the sum
and product evaluations, with residues and quotients retained. The
signature \(\mathrm{TRIT}=\{-1,0,+1\}\) distinguishes orientation and
regime; the \emph{Topological Phase Kernel} (TPK) composes evaluation
selection, transport and memory update to extend compatible states.
The same tree of histories supports cylinder measure, sheet transport,
oriented balance, incidence and memory complexes with their refinement maps.

The generated dodecaphasic register \(K\) links two realisations.
Its incidence determines a Witt flag which, with the specified gluing,
metric and chamber, leads to a Leech neighbour. The vertex-algebra
construction theorems applicable to this lattice identify the extensions
of orders two and three with \(V^\natural\), whose automorphism group is
the Monster; chosen isomorphisms transport products, grading and traces.
In the twelve-position representation of \(A_5\), the projection of
\(K\) onto the three-dimensional sector is nonzero and determines the
orthogonal reduction \(12\to11\to10\). The cyclic orbit of the register
identifies operators with stability bounds; bilateral balance provides
state and memory recovery under its isometric hypotheses.

Comparison of the stars of \(B_K\), \(B_\varphi\) and \(B_{\rm APP}\) distinguishes
the polarised signatures \((3,1,0)\), \((1,0,3)\) and \((1,1,2)\).
The ordered frame of the four supports
\(H^-_\alpha,H_e,H_\varphi,H_{\rm APP}\) has trivial stabiliser
in the Witt action on the twelve positions. Alongside the regional
residues, the numerical reading retains the integer quotients of
sum and difference: their composition satisfies a cocycle identity,
and their reductions supply additional algebraic components beyond
the affine evaluation of the inputs. These results specify the
complementary roles of incidence, orientation and carry memory
within the common extension.

The Weyl realisation of phase generates the matrix algebras at each
depth; full-fibre inclusions yield a uniformly hyperfinite algebra and
a tracial factor of type \(II_1\). The action section and angular pair
fix an oriented ellipse and a unique normalised radius. Integer-coordinate
exchange and radius inversion preserve its quadratic form and unitarily
intertwine the self-adjoint Hamiltonians, including their domains.
Transport of the residual relation ensures covariance under a change
of section. A screen morphism coordinates this duality with the
\(K\)-directed reduction and the isotropic lattice quotient \(26\to24\).

Composition of trajectories with boundary data retained defines an
additive action cocycle. Its extension towards M-theory specifies the
codomain of eleven-dimensional fields and the realisation maps that must
preserve action, grading and duality. Under the stated intertwining and
domain conditions, the supercharge identity is transported to the
isometric image. The conditions for a complete physical identification
are distinguished from these algebraic and operator-theoretic results.

The arithmetic preceding these realizations includes the APP defect
laws under changes of modulus and dimension, the invariant index-two
sublattice, and nonadic encoding with carry. The emitter determines
an alphabet of \(42\) blocks and explicit regional junctions; the ordered
register retains correlations distinct from its histogram. Quadratic
correlation proves the boundary-chart identity, and selection separates
saturation, dual neutrality, and orientation. The level-three rational
identity also establishes optimal uniform divisibility by \(3^9\) in
the formal power-series ring.
