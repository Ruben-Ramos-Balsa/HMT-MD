\section{Complete modal table for the prime-vacancy observer}
\label{v:f051:appendix:modal-table}

The following table preserves the thirty evaluations recorded by the finite
F051 witness at \(X=10^8\). Its columns are obtained from
\(S_k(X)=\sum_{p\leq X}(\log p)e^{2\pi i k p\delta}\), with
\(1\leq k\leq30\). They constitute numerical evidence for that execution
within the finite window; modal selection, asymptotic promotion, and the
internal construction of irreducibles remain defined by their respective
mechanisms. The unrounded data are delivered in
\path{RECUPERACION/F051/S_k_1_30_X1e8.csv}.

\paragraph{Numerical provenance.}
The historical package preserves \texttt{R\_vac\_powers.csv} through
\(10^8\) and \texttt{S\_k\_1\_30\_X1e8.csv}, but it does not contain the
program that produced the sweep. The documented independent reproduction
reaches \(10^6\); the later cutoffs and the thirty modes retain the status
of finite historical witnesses.
