\subsubsection{The seam term in the \(108\to1080\) concatenation}
\label{vi:subsubsec:costura-108-1080}

Prolongation of the chronological reading must retain the effective
predecessor of every event. Let
\(d^{(b)}=(d^{(b)}_1,\ldots,d^{(b)}_{108})\), \(0\le b<10\), be ten
consecutive words, and define
\[
 a_b=d^{(b)}_1,\qquad q_b=d^{(b)}_{108},\qquad q_{-1}=q_9.
\]
Let \(T_z^{\sqcup}\) be the sum of the ten readers evaluated separately,
using \(q_b\) as the cyclic predecessor of \(a_b\), and let
\(T_z^{\Vert}\) be the reader of the concatenated word, which uses
\(q_{b-1}\) as the effective predecessor of \(a_b\).

\begin{lemma}[Exact seam correction]
\label{vi:lem:costura-108-1080}
With \(\Delta T_z=T_z^{\Vert}-T_z^{\sqcup}\),
\begin{equation}
 \Delta T_z=
 \sum_{b=0}^{9}\mathbf1_{\{a_b=9\}}
 \bigl(j_2(q_{b-1})-j_2(q_b)\bigr).
 \label{vi:eq:delta-costura-tz}
\end{equation}
If crowns occur at \(t\equiv0\pmod{27}\), then
\begin{equation}
 \Delta C_z=0,
 \qquad
 \Delta k_{\Delta_4}=\Delta T_z.
 \label{vi:eq:delta-costura-k}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Away from the ten block beginnings, every event has the same predecessor
under both evaluations. If \(a_b\ne9\), the indicator defining \(z_t\)
also annihilates the possible predecessor difference. If \(a_b=9\), the
separate term is \(j_2(q_b)\), whereas the concatenated term is
\(j_2(q_{b-1})\); their difference is the corresponding summand in
\eqref{vi:eq:delta-costura-tz}. Summing the ten blocks proves the first
identity. The new block beginnings occupy positions
\(108b+1\equiv1\pmod{27}\), never a crown. Hence the seam does not
change \(C_z\), and the definition
\(k_{\Delta_4}=T_z-2C_z\) gives
\eqref{vi:eq:delta-costura-k}.
\end{proof}

The correction genuinely attains \(-2\). Take a single beginning
\(a_b=9\), with \(q_{b-1}=1\), \(q_b=2\), and require
\(a_r\ne9\) at every other beginning. Then
\(j_2(q_{b-1})-j_2(q_b)=-1-1=-2\). This word is a seam-reader control;
it is not promoted to a surviving route, particle, or physical signature.

Length \(1080\) repeats ten phase stations, not ten copies of an
unchanging depth. The correct transport is the ordered composition
\[
 \mathcal H_9\circ\mathcal H_8\circ\cdots\circ\mathcal H_0.
\]
Only an additional stationarity proof would justify replacing it by
\(\mathcal H_{108}^{10}\). The seam formula retains exactly the memory
that such a replacement could erase.
