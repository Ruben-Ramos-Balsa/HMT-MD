\subsection{Transport of the electronic section to the electroweak sector}
\label{amp-ew-seccion}

The central section in \eqref{md:hib:electron} jointly retains the
prefactor, the correction and the return memory. Its dependencies arise
from the APP sheets, the TRIT orientation and the route recorded by TPK;
the angular character and the towers subsequently act on multisections
of the same discrete structure of the continuum. Write
\begin{equation}
 r_e=R_{\rm act}^{\kappa_e},\qquad
 E_e^\beta=\mathcal U_E\varepsilon_e^{(0)}r_e,\qquad
 \kappa_e=80-54\alpha+6\Delta_4.
 \label{amp-ew-electron}
\end{equation}
Here $\mathcal U_E$ is the section of the energy line and $r_e$ denotes
the electronic return factor. The action decade remains in its own line;
the realised energy coordinate is transported exactly once.

The angular signatures in \eqref{amp-higgs-firmas} produce, in the same
chart, $E_s^\angle=E_e^\beta\exp(n_sx+s_sy/6)$. In particular,
\begin{equation}
 E_W^\angle=\mathcal U_E\varepsilon_e^{(0)}r_e
                  e^{94x-y/6},\qquad D=e^{-2x}.
 \label{amp-ew-w}
\end{equation}
This is the angular realisation of W. Refinements in another tower
retain their own factors when a composition uses them.

\begin{proposition}[Electronic scale and tree-level Fermi coupling]
In the tree-level electroweak realisation, with the energy coordinates
expressed in one unit and $g^2=4\pi\alpha/(1-D)$, the relations
$v=2E_W^\angle/g$ and $G_F^{(0)}=1/(\sqrt2v^2)$ give
\begin{equation}
 G_F^{(0)}=
 \frac{\pi\alpha\,e^{-188x+y/3}}
 {\sqrt2(1-D)\mathcal U_E^2(\varepsilon_e^{(0)})^2r_e^2}.
 \label{amp-ew-fermi}
\end{equation}
With the signatures, channels and energy chart fixed, the passage from
the basal to the returned stage satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \frac{(G_F^{(0)})_\beta}{(G_F^{(0)})_0}=r_e^{-2},\qquad
 \frac{v_\beta}{v_0}=r_e.
 \label{amp-ew-escala}
\end{equation}
The ratios $E_W^\angle/E_Z^\angle$, $E_H^\angle/E_W^\angle$ and
$\lambda_H=(g^2/8)(E_H^\angle/E_W^\angle)^2$ are independent of
this common factor $r_e$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Substitution of $v$ gives $G_F^{(0)}=g^2/[4\sqrt2(E_W^\angle)^2]$.
Using the expression for $g^2$ and squaring \eqref{amp-ew-w}
yields \eqref{amp-ew-fermi}: the coefficients $188$ and $1/3$ arise
respectively from $2\cdot94$ and $2/6$. Replacing $E_e^0$ by
$E_e^\beta=r_eE_e^0$ multiplies all angular energies by $r_e$.
The definition of $v$ retains its first power; that of $G_F^{(0)}$
retains its inverse square. Each energy ratio cancels the same positive
factor, and the formula for $\lambda_H$ preserves this cancellation.
The couplings $g,g'$ depend on $\alpha,x$ and remain fixed in this
comparison.
\end{proof}

The quantity $G_F^{(0)}$ has inverse-energy-squared dimension.
The declared tree-level realisation specifies the physical scope of this
composition; radiative corrections have their own subsequent operators
and order. Comparing the two stages of the reader retains their charts
and signatures, rather than postulating independent temporal variation
of the correlated constants.

\subsection{Preservation of route refinements}
\label{amp-ew-refinamiento}
Composition with the complete law preserves the character and tower
of each route. For two comparable positive eigensections in the same
chart and with the same reader $r\in\{D,\Omega\}$, let
\begin{equation}
 \rho_{W/e}^{\,r}
 =\frac{\chi_{\rm full}^{\,r}(\sigma_W,\eta_W)}
        {\chi_{\rm full}^{\,r}(\sigma_e,\eta_e)}
 R_{\rm act}^{\kappa_r(\Gamma_W)-\kappa_e}.
 \label{amp-ew-razon-completa}
\end{equation}
The corresponding energy is $E_W=E_e^\beta\rho_{W/e}^{\,r}$.
In the Fermi realisation compatible with that section, one obtains
\begin{equation}
 G_F^{(0)}=
 \frac{\pi\alpha}
 {\sqrt2(1-D)(E_e^\beta)^2(\rho_{W/e}^{\,r})^2}.
 \label{amp-ew-fermi-completo}
\end{equation}
The proof substitutes the complete ratio into the preceding quadratic
identity. In the explicit angular chart, that ratio takes the form in
\eqref{amp-ew-w}; in a refined realisation it also retains the tower
and reader differences in \eqref{md:hib:cocientes}.
If $E_s=E_e^\beta\chi_sf_s$, then
$E_s/E_t=(\chi_s/\chi_t)(f_s/f_t)$. This identity shows exactly
which factor cancels and which species-specific information remains.

\subsection{Mass congruence and spectral inversion without commutation}
\label{amp-ew-congruencia}
Let $\widehat M^0>0$ be the basal operator on a finite positive fibre,
including its character and towers, and let $\widehat B^r$ be the
self-adjoint return reader. Action transport produces
$C=R_{\rm act}^{\widehat B^r/2}>0$ and the law
$\widehat M^\beta=C\widehat M^0C$.

\begin{proposition}[Operator reciprocity of the action length]
For $\hbar_a,c>0$ in the same dimensional chart,
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 (\widehat M^\beta)^{-1}
    &=C^{-1}(\widehat M^0)^{-1}C^{-1},\\
 \widehat L_C^\beta:=\frac{\hbar_a}{c}(\widehat M^\beta)^{-1}
    &=C^{-1}\widehat L_C^0C^{-1},\qquad
 \widehat L_C^0=\frac{\hbar_a}{c}(\widehat M^0)^{-1}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{amp-ew-inversa-operador}
\end{equation}
These identities allow $[C,\widehat M^0]\ne0$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Positivity of $C$ implies invertibility. For $u\ne0$,
$\langle u,C\widehat M^0Cu\rangle
=\langle Cu,\widehat M^0Cu\rangle>0$, so the final operator is
also invertible. Direct multiplication in both orders gives
\[
 (C\widehat M^0C)\bigl(C^{-1}(\widehat M^0)^{-1}C^{-1}\bigr)=I,
 \qquad
 \bigl(C^{-1}(\widehat M^0)^{-1}C^{-1}\bigr)(C\widehat M^0C)=I.
\]
Multiplication by $\hbar_a/c$ proves the second identity. The spectral
decomposition of the final operator,
$\widehat M^\beta=\sum_jm_jP_j$ with $m_j>0$, yields
$\widehat L_C^\beta=\sum_j\hbar_a/(cm_j)P_j$. The reciprocal function
is injective on the positive eigenvalues, preserving their final
projectors and multiplicities.
\end{proof}

Congruence may change the basal operator's projectors; spectral
inversion preserves those of the final operator. The null sector is
outside the domain of this inversion. A preparation occupying several
eigenspaces retains its spectral measure and multiplicities.
In rank one, the identity recovers the electronic length
$\bar\lambda_{C,e}=\hbar_ac/E_e^\beta$ and then
$a_{{\rm B},e}=\bar\lambda_{C,e}/\alpha$. The result connects the
electronic scale, the electroweak response and the action length through
explicit operations on their shared structures.
