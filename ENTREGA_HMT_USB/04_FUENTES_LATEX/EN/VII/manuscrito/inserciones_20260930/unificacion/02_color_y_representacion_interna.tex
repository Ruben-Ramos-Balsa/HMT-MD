\section{Chromatic residue and internal representation of the joint action}
\label{hmtcuatro:color:seccion}

The APP--TRIT--TPK chain preserves the enriched state, its routes, orientations, sheets and memory within the joint discrete structure of the continuum. The residue read below is a finite readout of that state; it neither replaces it nor resets its history. The phase coordinates, including the complex unit and the regional coordinate \(\pi\), are HMT outputs received before this algebraic realization. The representation is constructed on the residual support with the declared chart, orientation and phase character. A gauge group is not inferred from the number of labels alone.

\subsection{Residual support and preserved orientation}
\label{hmtcuatro:color:residuo}

In the positional copy of APP, let
\[
 I_9=\{1,\ldots,9\},\qquad
 \mathcal C=I_9\times I_9,\qquad
 \mathcal C_q=\{(r,c)\in\mathcal C:r\text{ is even}\}.
\]
The residual observable and the cellular inversion are
\[
 \kappa_{\rm col}(r,c)=r-c\pmod3,\qquad
 \rho_{\rm c}(r,c)=(10-r,10-c).
\]
The symbol \(\kappa_{\rm col}\) does not denote the gravitational coefficient of the action. This observable is lifted to the persistent sections through projection to the atlas and preserves the remaining records; it does not select a cell by its evaluated mass.

\begin{proposition}[Ternary fibres and chromatic balance]
\label{hmtcuatro:color:balance} The functional \(\kappa_3:\mathbb F_3^2\to\mathbb F_3\), \(\kappa_3(r,c)=r-c\), has three affine fibres of cardinality three. On \(\mathcal C_q\), the three residues have cardinalities \(12,12,12\). Cellular inversion negates the residue.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For each \(j\in\mathbb F_3\), the fibre is \(\{(c+j,c):c\in\mathbb F_3\}\), a translate of the kernel \(\{(c,c)\}\). In \(\mathcal C_q\) there are four even values of \(r\); for each one, \(c=1,\ldots,9\) runs three times through each class modulo three. Each residue has \(4\cdot3=12\) representatives. Finally,
\[
 \kappa_{\rm col}(\rho_{\rm c}(r,c))
 =(10-r)-(10-c)=-(r-c)\pmod3.
\]
The zero class is fixed, and classes one and two are exchanged.
\end{proof}

This balance does not identify flavour, depth and colour. Nor does it assert that the three lines to be constructed are three complete states: the routes and memory sharing a residual reading remain in the enriched fibre.

\subsection{Qutrit chart, Weyl pair and colour algebra}
\label{hmtcuatro:color:weyl}

We fix the chart
\[
 V_{\rm col}=\mathbb C[\mathbb F_3]\simeq\mathbb C^3,
 \qquad (e_0,e_1,e_2),\qquad
 \omega_3=e^{2\pi i/3},
\]
with orthonormal delta basis, orientation \(j\mapsto j+1\) and character \(j\mapsto\omega_3^j\). The operators are
\[
 Xe_j=e_{j+1\bmod3},\qquad Ze_j=\omega_3^j e_j,
 \qquad X^3=Z^3=I,\quad ZX=\omega_3XZ.
\]
The last equality is checked by applying both sides to each \(e_j\). The map from the atlas to this chart is
\[
 (r,c)\longmapsto\mathbb C e_{\kappa_{\rm col}(r,c)}.
\]
Thus, \(X\) permutes the lines and \(Z\) records their phases. Changing the affine chart and jointly transporting the basis, orientation and character conjugates the operators by the corresponding unitary change-of-basis matrix. It is not equivalent to changing just one label while keeping the other data fixed.

\begin{theorem}[Matrix algebra and compact real form]
\label{hmtcuatro:color:algebra} The nine operators \(W_{ab}=X^aZ^b\), \((a,b)\in\mathbb F_3^2\), form an orthogonal basis of \(\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(V_{\rm col})\). The eight nonzero indices linearly span \(\mathfrak{sl}_3(\mathbb C)\), whose real form of anti-Hermitian matrices is \(\mathfrak{su}(3)\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If \(a\ne0\), the matrix \(X^aZ^b\) has no diagonal entries. If \(a=0\), its trace is \(1+\omega_3^b+\omega_3^{2b}\), which is zero for \(b\ne0\) and three for \(b=0\). The Weyl relation then gives
\[
 \operatorname{tr}(W_{ab}^{\dagger}W_{cd})
 =3\delta_{ac}\delta_{bd}.
\]
Independence and dimension nine prove the first assertion; the remaining eight form a basis of the traceless subspace. Moreover,
\[
 W_{ab}^{\dagger}=\omega_3^{ab}W_{-a,-b}.
\]
The eight nonzero indices form four adjoint pairs. Taking \(\mathcal R=\{(1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(1,2)\}\), the matrices
\[
 A_u=W_u-W_u^{\dagger},\qquad
 B_u=i(W_u+W_u^{\dagger}),\qquad u\in\mathcal R,
\]
are anti-Hermitian and traceless. Their real independence follows from the complex independence of the eight \(W_u,W_{-u}\): in each pair, the coefficients of a vanishing real combination force both real coefficients to zero. They form an eight-dimensional real basis. Therefore,
\[
 \{A\in\mathfrak{sl}_3(\mathbb C):-A^{\dagger}=A\}
 =\mathfrak{su}(3).
\]
\end{proof}

The fixed Hermitian form and complex orientation determine the realization group
\[
 SU(V_{\rm col})=\{U:U^{\dagger}U=I,\ \det U=1\}.
\]
Its tangent algebra is exactly the preceding one: differentiating both equations at the identity gives \(A^{\dagger}=-A\) and \(\operatorname{tr}A=0\); conversely, \(e^{tA}\) satisfies both. Every group element can be written as the exponential of a matrix in that algebra. Indeed, unitarily diagonalize \(U\) with eigenvalues \(e^{i\theta_j}\); since the determinant is one, \(\sum_j\theta_j=2\pi n\). Subtracting \(2\pi n\) from one of the angles does not alter \(U\) and produces a traceless anti-Hermitian logarithm. This continuous realization is not identified with the finite group generated solely by \(X,Z\). It preserves the compact form and introduces no value for the strong coupling.

\subsection{Chromatic transport and independent internal factors}
\label{hmtcuatro:color:transporte}

In a finite oriented cellularization compatible with the routes, we assign \(U_e\in SU(V_{\rm col})\) and \(U_{\bar e}=U_e^{-1}\). For \(g_v\in SU(V_{\rm col})\),
\[
 U_e^g=g_{t(e)}U_eg_{s(e)}^{-1},\qquad
 \psi_v^g=g_v\psi_v.
\]
By substitution,
\[
 U_e^g\psi_{s(e)}^g-\psi_{t(e)}^g
 =g_{t(e)}(U_e\psi_{s(e)}-\psi_{t(e)}).
\]
The opposite sign, used in \(D_T\), has the same covariance. An oriented product around a face transforms by conjugation with the gauge at the base vertex: the factors at intermediate vertices cancel consecutively. In particular, for \(\beta\ge0\),
\[
 S_{\rm col}^{(\mathcal K,\beta)}[U]
 =\frac\beta3\sum_f(3-\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}U_f)
\]
is invariant and nonnegative. Invariance uses the cyclic trace; positivity follows from \(\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}U_f\le3\). The parameter and cellularization retain their status as data of this realization. This proof does not determine them by comparison with chromodynamic values.

The matter of the joint action uses this factor, not another partition of the atlas. For left-handed quarks, its internal fibre has factors \(V_{\rm col}\otimes\mathbb C^2\otimes F\), where \(F\) preserves families; for each right-handed type, \(V_{\rm col}\otimes F\) is used. The colour factor is trivial in leptons. The spinorial factor is retained separately. For colour matrices \(A\) and weak matrices \(B\), we have
\[
 [A\otimes I,I\otimes B]=0,
\]
since both products are \(A\otimes B\). The hypercharge characters act as scalars on each multiplet and also commute. This identity allows the internal connections and the spin connection to be composed in the same derivative without confusing colour, chirality and flavour.

The Yukawa maps of the subsequent action are the identity on the chromatic factor and maps on the Higgs--family factors. Therefore \(Y_q(H)(g_{\rm col}\otimes I)
=(g_{\rm col}\otimes I)Y_q(H)\). Weak and hypercharge compatibility is proved there with their explicit columns and weights; it does not follow from the census \(12+12+12\). This preserves the residual--qutrit--algebra--transport chain required by the common action, without asserting a Yang--Mills quantum limit in this background or replacing its source documents.
