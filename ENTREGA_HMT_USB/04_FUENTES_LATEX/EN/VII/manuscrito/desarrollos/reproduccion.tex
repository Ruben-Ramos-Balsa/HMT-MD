\section{Sources, preservation, and reproduction}
The digital supplement preserves the complete cumulative document, its
thirty-nine sources, the verification programs, and the corresponding
records. The approved narrative and its continuation are retained
verbatim in that archive. This edition recasts their conversational
form as an academic exposition and records the correspondence between
sections, while preserving the originals.

The antecedents originate in the series sources of 21 September 2026.
The formal kernel is incorporated from its full documentary dependency
closure in the constitutive article; the developments on action,
incidence, mass, and temperature are preserved together with their
proofs. The manifest identifies each file, extracted range, and hash.
These data document provenance and preservation, while the proofs in
the body establish the mathematical scope of the results.

The programs for composition of constants, hybridization, and
compatibility check exact identities and finite examples. Numerical
diagnostics are distinguished from rational checks. The proofs of
monotonicity, convergence, inversion, and passage to the limit remain
developed in the text: the executable examples complement those proofs.

Compilation uses LuaLaTeX. The included sources and figures allow the
PDF to be reproduced independently of the author's original
directories. The package instructions specify the compilation sequence
and how to execute the checks.
