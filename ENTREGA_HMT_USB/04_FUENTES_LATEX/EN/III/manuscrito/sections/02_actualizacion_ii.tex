\section{Dodecaphasic update and expressed memory}
\label{sec:eta}
Let $d_t$ be the integer direction code preserved by a TPK trace.
This code and the cardinal direction of the cursor are distinct coordinates.
The twelve windows
$E_m=\{9m+1,\ldots,9m+9\}$, $0\le m<12$, partition the 108 steps of the register.
The orientation is
\[
\Sigma_{12}=(+,+,+,-,-,-,+,+,+,-,-,-).
\]
The event of a window is the output
\begin{equation}
a_m=\Sigma_{12}(m)
\sum_{t\in E_m}\mathbf1_{\{2,4,6,8\}}(d_t).
\label{eq:events}
\end{equation}
The notation $a_m$ avoids confusing this event with Euler's number.
The source register also preserves the TRIT orientation, signature,
carry and corresponding history segment (see \ref{sec:k-registro}).

\begin{proposition}[Increment produced by transport]
Let $\mathbf e_m$ be the basis of $\R^{12}$ and
$S\mathbf e_m=\mathbf e_{m+1\pmod{12}}$. If
$z_{m+1}=z_m+a_m\mathbf e_m$ and $y_m=S^{-m}z_m$, then
\begin{equation}
y_{m+1}=S^{-1}y_m+\eta_m,
\qquad
\eta_m=a_mS^{-1}\mathbf e_0.
\label{eq:eta}
\end{equation}
After a complete winding,
\begin{equation}
y_{12}=y_0+\sum_{m=0}^{11}a_m\mathbf e_m.
\label{eq:cycle}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Multiplying the update of $z_m$ by $S^{-(m+1)}$ gives
\[
y_{m+1}=S^{-1}y_m+a_mS^{-(m+1)}\mathbf e_m
=S^{-1}y_m+a_mS^{-1}\mathbf e_0.
\]
Upon iteration, the coefficient of event $a_m$ is
$S^{-(12-m)}\mathbf e_0=\mathbf e_m$. Since $S^{12}=I$,
\eqref{eq:cycle} follows.
\end{proof}

Thus, $\eta_m$ is not a free force chosen to impose a
conservation law: it is a representation of the TPK event. Equality of phase
after a winding does not cancel the accumulated events. This readout
preserves the counts, whereas the complete register also preserves their
order and provenance. We distinguish $\eta_m$, a vector increment, from
$\eta_{\rm ret}$, the scalar action mantissa, and from $\eta_{\rm el}$,
the shape parameter to be introduced in Section~\ref{sec:ellipse}.

\subsection{Quadratic balance}
We define
\[
\nabla_3=I-S^3,\quad \nabla_4=I-S^4,\quad
B=\nabla_3^{\mathsf T}\nabla_3+\nabla_4^{\mathsf T}\nabla_4,\qquad
\mathcal M(y)=\tfrac12 y^{\mathsf T}By.
\]
The oriented readouts of the signed register satisfy
$(D_jz)_m=z_m-z_{m+j}$; with the convention
$S\mathbf e_m=\mathbf e_{m+1}$, one has
$D_j=I-S^{-j}=\nabla_j^{\mathsf T}$. Both directions produce the same
quadratic form $B$, since powers of $S$ commute.
We have $S^{\mathsf T}BS=B$ and $B_{00}=4$. Substituting
\eqref{eq:eta} yields exactly
\begin{equation}
\mathcal M(y_{m+1})-\mathcal M(y_m)
=a_m(By_m)_0+2a_m^2.
\label{mem:balance}
\end{equation}
Likewise, the charge $q(y)=\sum_jy_j$ satisfies
$q(y_{m+1})-q(y_m)=a_m$.
The proof consists of expanding the quadratic polynomial after removing the
permutation $S^{-1}$. No metrological value enters this calculation.
The form $\mathcal M$ measures this register; its interpretation as a
specific physical energy would require the corresponding dimensional readout.
