\HMTNarrativeHeading{The form preserved by the second projection}

The exceptional realization develops the incidence that accompanies
numerical generation. Each prefix transmits boundary relations compatible
with truncations. The Paley encoding preserves the word and its image;
the Witt design organizes its supports; the lift towards Golay introduces
binary incidence with its markings. The subsequent development constructs
the corresponding lattice and operator realizations.

Incidence stars make it possible to observe this link. The coupling
register determines a tetrad. The hexads that contain it distinguish four
complementary pairs, and a signature selects the marked hexad. In the
figure, the connections represent operations between sets. The lift to
octads preserves this configuration and distinguishes a fifth transverse
octad. The five numerical regions thus maintain a correspondence with
specified incidence relations.

Equality of a ternary reading may bring together regions whose
orientation, multiplicity, and position remain distinct. These data
participate in the exceptional correspondence. The second projection
preserves precisely those relations that the value reading treats
differently. Compatibility between the two is expressed on the common
enriched state and its refinement maps.

M-theory and T-duality are presented through the dimensional realizations
and their compatibility conditions. The Monster action has its carrier in
the space constructed for it. The numerical readers, finite
configurations, lattices, and representations receive distinct domains
within a single genealogy. This precision makes it possible to follow how
an incidence structure is transmitted to its subsequent consequences.

The following part preserves the constructions, tables, and proofs that
make this development effective. The narrative returns each realization
to the guiding question: which relations survive projection, and how are
they recovered in the object they reach? The conservation of a Gram form,
the covariance of an encoding, and the invariance of an action will have
their own proofs. Their articulation supplies the geometrical content of
the evolutionary conservation of unity.
