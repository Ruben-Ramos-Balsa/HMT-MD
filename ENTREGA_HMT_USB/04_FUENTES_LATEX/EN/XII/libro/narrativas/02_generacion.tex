\HMTNarrativeHeading{The depth of a number}

Coinductive generation organizes the determination of a number through
compatible prolongations. A stage produces a prefix and preserves the
conditions for continuing it. Depth can increase while maintaining agreement
among all previous readings. The value emerges through the realization of
this compatibility; the history also preserves the way in which precision
was obtained.

The regions of the circular, golden, and exponential coordinates arise from
the same APP--TRIT--TPK development. Their readers perform distinct functions
and share the structure that makes it possible to relate them. The triadic
extension of Euler makes the realizations of the regimes explicit; the
areal--volumetric calendar determines self-scaling; regional emissions and
their boundaries preserve the conditions of evaluation. The succession of
these steps makes explicit the construction order of the coordinates that
subsequently enter into alpha.

The vacancy calendar makes it possible to understand a particular feature of
refinement. History advances with every transition, while the capacity of a
reading may remain fixed. The word that records these transitions
distinguishes acquired capacity from preserved vacancy. Its balance counts
both contributions. The regularity of the calendar and the aperiodicity of
its itinerary describe related aspects of one and the same evolution.

Monodromy extends this distinction. Returning to a phase means recognizing a
position in the cycle; recovering a history means also restoring its turns,
orientation, and registers. The regional mirror preserves the axis and the
aggregate of the two coordinates it exchanges, while reversing their
difference. This datum makes it possible to reconstruct the distribution.
The nonadic figure locates the phases and their operations; its marks are
positions in the calendar, with a semantics different from that of the real
value of a constant.

The fifth boundary, the subsequent regions, and the return are therefore
explained through their actions on the state. The regional figures show
simultaneously the coincidence of a reading and the differentiation of its
antecedents. This differentiation transmits to the exceptional realization
the incidence information it uses. Numerical generation thus continues the
double projection introduced at the outset: each reading obtains its value
from a structure whose organization remains available to the other
realizations.
