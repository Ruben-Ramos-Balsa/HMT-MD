\HMTNarrativeHeading{Proportion, refinement, and restoration}

The holographic extreme and mean ratio here brings together arithmetic
completion and operator recovery. Refinement with carry redistributes
a unit among components and preserves the normalized sum. Euclidean
division, applied with the declared depth and carry convention,
recovers the previous labels. Conservation and the inverse thus
receive concrete operations.

The ternary norm supplies the integer seventy-three and its algebraic
and lattice interpretation. The relation to decimal completion is
expressed through exact identities. The bilateral balance extends the
analysis to two isometric transports between spaces of arbitrary
dimension. The cross terms cancel in a weighted combination and
restore the initial squared norm.

The nonadic specialization determines the initial allocation of
weights: seventy-two over seventy-three and one over seventy-three.
An orthogonal mixing then produces the two normalized channels. When
the transports admit the declared relative unitary operator, the
effective reader combines the observable with its conjugate; its
invariance is then equivalent to commutation with that relative
operator. Transport of the complete state simultaneously preserves
the relations that each observable allows to be evaluated.

The archiving policy prolongs this balance. Successive memories and
the terminal component maintain the whole at finite depth. The
uniform estimate of the terminal component makes it possible to
obtain an infinite archive recoverable through the adjoint of the
isometric operator. The bounds describe the stability of this
recovery as depth increases.

The coupling constant participates in the same construction. Its
orbit and Gram form make it possible to identify operators; the
archive isometry preserves these responses and their bounds. Label
refinement lifts to a unitary transport and is composed with the
bilateral balance. Unity thus preserves its arithmetic content,
its linear realization, and the possibility of restoration. This is
the operational culmination of the guiding argument: a generated
structure admits transformations that redistribute its reading and
preserve the data for its recovery.
