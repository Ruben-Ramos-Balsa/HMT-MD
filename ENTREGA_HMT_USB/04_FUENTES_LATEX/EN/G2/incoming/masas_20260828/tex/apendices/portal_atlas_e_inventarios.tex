\section{Public projection of the internal record of the routes}

Each route has \(53\) fields in the unified register and \(57\) fields in the
enriched register. The scientific record publishes \(49\) fields. This
projection does not eliminate information: the consistency states of the route,
of the gluing, and of tower prolongation, together with the \(5\) raw
components of the signature, remain in the complete registers and are gathered in the
published invariants. Therefore, the publication map is a
typed projection
\[
  \mathcal R_{57}\longrightarrow\mathcal F_{49},
\]
accompanied by the complete register \(\mathcal R_{53}\) and by the
enriched register \(\mathcal R_{57}\); it is not a reduction of the scientific domain.

\section{Correspondence between the genealogical domain and the metrological inventories}

The material domain is constructed from \(81\) cells, \(56\) route
families, \(13\) multisections, and \(324\) oriented routes. Its complete deployment
is found in the
\hyperref[chap:atlas-genealogico-exhaustivo-324-rutas]{atlas of \(324\)
routes}, with a complete card and \(49\) fields by route. The external censuses
of contrast are preserved in the
\hyperref[chap:inventario-universal-471-masas-384-anchuras]{inventory of
\(471\) masses and \(384\) widths}. The atlas constructs the fibers and their
operators; the censuses do not retrospectively select a route or
redefine the domain of the law.
