\section{Exact correspondence between the canonical record of \(53\) fields, the enriched record of \(57\) fields, and the \(49\) invariants of each card}
\label{sec:correspondencia-atlas-53-57-49-sucesor}

The \(324\) oriented routes have two complete proprietary registers and
one scientific publication per record. The registers of \(53\) and \(57\)
fields overlap without being identified: their union contains \(65\) distinct
identifiers. The publication column selects exactly \(49\) invariants
for each record, while the remaining data remain preserved in the
reproducibility layers. Therefore, projection onto the record neither eliminates
information from the domain nor reduces the atlas.
