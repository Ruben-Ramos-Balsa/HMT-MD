\section{Typed heterogeneity and catalog coverage}

The catalog collects all the layers located without converting them into the same observable:
\begin{itemize}
\item the \(81\) cells, \(56\) families, and \(13\) multisections form the
simple domain;
\item \(81+432+1728+372\) counts identities, expressions, and arrows of the
compositional grammar, not physical particles;
\item the complete signatures, the N/E/S/O projections, and the reduced charts
      remain as specialized controls of the evaluation, without limiting
      the exhaustive domain or retrospectively selecting its routes;
\item neutrinos, the photon, the gluon, Fibonacci, Ising, and Majorana retain their
own observables.
\end{itemize}

Scientific completeness consists in omitting no domain and in misrepresenting the type of no row. The character law is unique; each simple section or composite register is evaluated through \cref{eq:firmas-simples-compuestas-rev6,eq:ley-masa-todos-registros-rev6}. The external value appears at the end, together with the object class and the reader that evaluates it. The comparison thus ceases to be a list of successes and becomes a verifiable reading of the genealogy.
