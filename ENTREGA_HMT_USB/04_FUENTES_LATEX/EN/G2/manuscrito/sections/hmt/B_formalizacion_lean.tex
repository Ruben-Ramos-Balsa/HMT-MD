\chapter{Formal Verification of Finite Algebraic Identities}
\label{app:lean}

The following finite equalities are formalized in Lean and decided by
reduction of the logical core, without additional external axioms. The theorems
prove:
\begin{longtable}{p{.40\textwidth}p{.50\textwidth}}
\toprule
Theorem & Contents\\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{aw\_symmetric}
& symmetry of the Paley--Witt matrix \(A_W\);\\
\texttt{aw\_squared\_minus\_identity}
& \(A_W^2=-I_6\) over \(\mathbb F_3\);\\
\texttt{x\_sq\_add\_one\_has\_no\_root} criterion
& irreducibility of \(x^2+1\) over \(\mathbb F_3\);\\
\texttt{dual\_has\_nonzero\_nilpotent} record
& existence of a nilpotent in the parabolic algebra;\\
\texttt{split\_has\_nontrivial\_idempotent}
& existence of a nontrivial idempotent in the hyperbolic algebra;\\
\texttt{canonical\_charge}
& \(\sum_{m=1}^{12}K_m=6263\);\\
identifier \texttt{hadamard\_orbit\_one/two/three}
& The three Hadamard orbits produce the published dodecaphasic state.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

The formalization takes the matrices and the dodecaphase vector as typed
data and verifies their algebraic consequences. The construction of the
signed state and the enumeration of the (104\,976) seeds belong to the preceding
combinatorial theorems and are not replaced by this formal reduction.
