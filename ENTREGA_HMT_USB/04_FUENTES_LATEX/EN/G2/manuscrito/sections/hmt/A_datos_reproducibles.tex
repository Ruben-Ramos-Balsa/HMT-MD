\chapter{Finite Catalogs and Certificates of Reproducibility}
\label{app:datos-reproducibles}

The following finite objects allow the verifications to be repeated without
introducing production notation into the mathematical exposition. Each control
preserves its corresponding domain, operation, invariant, and
falsifier.

\section{Reproducibility of Certificates and Exact Obstructions of Finite Constructions}

The controls use integer or rational arithmetic whenever the object
permits it. The analytical bounds use intervals with directed rounding and
separate the symbolic proof from the decimal comparison. Each run
reconstructs the objects from the declared seeds, verifies the incidence and
naturality identities, and checks the outputs against an independent
arithmetic implementation.

The native-product control rejects every dependence on a decimal evaluation, an external factorization, a sieve, a table of zeros, or a target constant. The nonadic control rejects identification of the ambient fiber of \(729\) elements with the cardinality of a space of histories. The phase control rejects \(g(K+9)=g(K)\) as proof of equality of states. The metrological control rejects every route selection performed after an observed magnitude has been consulted.

A discrepancy among an identity in the text, the finite catalog, and its
independent control refutes the corresponding certification. This rule replaces
no proof: it fixes a second verification channel for the finite objects
that intervene in it.
