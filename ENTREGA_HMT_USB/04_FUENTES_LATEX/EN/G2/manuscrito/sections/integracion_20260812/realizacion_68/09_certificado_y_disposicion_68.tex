\section[Physical-Dimensional Implementation Controls]{Domain and Obstructions of Physical and Dimensional Realizations}
\label{sec:int68-certificado-historico}

The physical-dimensional realization is subject to ten families of control:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item cylindrical concentration, unit mass, and finite moments;
 \item spectra \((9,5)\) of the APP block and \((7,3)\) of the memory block;
 \item identity \(\tanh^2(2\log\varphi)=5/9\);
 \item antiunitarity in the stated finite model;
 \item Poincare semidirect-product law;
 \item constitutive identities of the vacuum;
 \item split norm of the null modes and material closure;
 \item selector \(\theta_*=(54/\pi)^2\) and self-dual fixed point;
 \item control CGLMP values;
 \item invariants \((396,729,43,387)\) of the nonadic filtration and
       domain \((81,56,13,324)\), with separate null sector, of the General
       Law of Masses.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{controlbox}[title={Exact scope of the controls}]
The ten families verify identities, finite domains, and reproducible
examples. They do not replace complete functoriality of the four
components, do not by themselves construct the common domain, do not prove the
smooth Cartan prolongation, and do not promote open physical interfaces to
internal theorems. The discrete realization retains its principal proof
in \cref{sec:int68-realizacion-fisica-discreta}.
\end{controlbox}

\subsection{Functorial dependencies of the spectral, solenoidal, gauge, cohomological, and determinantal realizations}

The preceding controls apply only after APP, TRIT, TPK,
the common enriched state, the double projection, and the dimensional line have been constructed. The
cylindrical concentration and finite spectrum prove properties of the
realization; they do not retrospectively select the state. The periodicity of
108 phases, the atlas (81/56/13/324), the constitutive involution, and the
cosmological programs retain separate domains and falsifiers.

The Dirac, von Neumann, CGLMP, Cartan, and Poincare identities are used
as typed controls of the corresponding intertwiner. A numerical
coincidence without the map specifying the domain, codomain, and action on generators does not
constitute a physical realization.
