\section{Exact coefficients of the polynomial}

The construction produces
\[
 \det(tI-CC^*)
 =t^9-\frac{50}{21}t^8+\frac{124}{63}t^7
  -\frac23t^6+\frac5{63}t^5,
 \tag{GMC-A.1}
\]
and coincides coefficient by coefficient with
\[
 t^5(t-1)(t-5/7)(t-1/3)^2.
 \tag{GMC-A.2}
\]
The verification constructs \(CC^*\) from the exact cardinalities of the
fibre intersections and applies Faddeev--LeVerrier over \(\mathbb Q\); it does not
depend on a numerical diagonalization.

\section{Obstructions to the identification of undemonstrated equivalences in the matrix bridge}

The finite certificate does not replace nonadic filtration to arbitrary depth, the
General Mass Law, or the Planck derivations. Nor does it prove by
itself APP--TRIT--TPK faithfulness of every cyclic QMS, complete equivariance
of the calibration \(\Xi\), identification of TPK memory with an
auxiliary Stinespring system, infinite divisibility of an MPS channel, or
categorical universality. Each of these assertions retains its principal
residence and its own falsifiers.
