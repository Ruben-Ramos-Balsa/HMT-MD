\section{Cofinal certificate of the Gauss--Kuzmin--Wirsing spectrum over the nonadic hierarchy}
\label{sec:p3-final:cierre-gkw}

For Gauss--Kuzmin--Wirsing, let \(P_N\) be the certified family of
truncations on the declared function space, and define
\[
 \Pi_m=P_{9^m}.
\]
The hierarchy \((\Pi_m)\) is a cofinal subsequence; the uniform bounds,
projector convergence, and absence of spectral contamination are
inherited from the family \((P_N)\). A different cylindrical discretization must
provide its intertwiner with \((P_{9^m})\).
