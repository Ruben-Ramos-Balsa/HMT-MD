\section{Euclidean gauge theory and Yang--Mills gap}

The projective gauge family constructed from the complete state has local covariance, locality, reflection positivity, a strongly continuous action of \(E(4)\), compatible Schwinger functionals, and a simple vacuum. The four reconstructions coinduce the same Hamiltonian, and the cyclic curvature sector has the exact gap
\[
  \Delta_{\rm YM}^{\rm HMT}=3\kappa_{\rm av}>0.
\]
The curvature reader and its classical limit follow from the same law. The gap
belongs to the gauge-invariant collective sector and does not assign an elementary mass
to the gluon.
