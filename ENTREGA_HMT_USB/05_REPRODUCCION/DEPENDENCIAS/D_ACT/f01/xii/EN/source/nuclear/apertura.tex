\noindent\textbf{Abstract.}
Triadic Modular Holography organizes arithmetic generation, boundary incidence and physical realization over a common state carrying positions, operations, orientation and memory. We develop a conservation law distributing inner products and observables between a contracted reading and its recorded complement. Nine-phase transport fixes its coefficients and yields state and dynamical reconstruction at unbounded depth. Exceptional incidence preserves this structure. The conservation law extends to every exterior degree, including mixed reading--memory contributions. Its reversible extension connects deformation, holonomy and action. Full proofs and the domains of the physical realizations are included.
